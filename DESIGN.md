# Design System: ARK Web Services

## Direction

The site keeps Ark's understated black, white, and gray identity. Typography and open space lead; hairline dividers organize the page; the existing Montserrat Alternates face identifies headings. No decorative gradients, blue accents, fictional results, or stock case-study imagery are used.

## Colors and typography

Use the warm near-white paper (`#f7f7f6`), near-black ink (`#111111`), and readable muted gray (`#555555`). Headings use self-hosted Montserrat Alternates; body and controls use the established DM Sans/system stack. Links, focus rings, and primary buttons remain dark, with visible contrast and keyboard focus.

## Layout and interaction

Keep the wide, open layout, strong typography, and thin rules from the current Ark direction. Service rows link to their own scope pages. At narrow widths, navigation, process steps, quotes, and contact options stack without horizontal overflow. Contact uses direct mailto and telephone links until inquiry storage and delivery are configured; it must not show a success state for a message that was not sent.

## Evidence and assets

Use Ark's favicon, licensed Montserrat font files, and the two existing client testimonial portraits. Show only approved client quotes. Portfolio/case-study sections remain absent until real work, metrics, and image permissions are supplied.
