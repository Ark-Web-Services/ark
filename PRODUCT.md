# ARK Web Services

## Product brief

ARK Web Services is a Charlotte-based web studio serving service businesses locally and nationwide. The site helps prospective clients understand four project types: WordPress rescue, website redesign, custom development, and one-time SEO foundations. Its primary action is a free site review request.

The site uses truthful business information and client feedback already present in the Ark source. It does not publish unverified case studies, performance metrics, rankings, client names, or project outcomes. Service pricing is scoped individually and shared before work begins.

## Core visitor paths

- Understand the work and request a site review from the homepage.
- Compare the four service types and read their scope.
- Learn how projects are reviewed, scoped, and delivered.
- Contact ARK by email or phone.
- Review the site's privacy and analytics choices.

## Operational constraints

Inquiry capture is disabled until production D1 storage, Turnstile, and email delivery are ready. Contact links remain available. Google Analytics 4 is configured for the Ark property and must load only after explicit acceptance. Analytics events must not include form fields, names, email addresses, or message contents.

Cloudflare Pages project/resource setup, domain DNS, and deployment are managed outside this repository. Do not claim rankings, leads, load-speed improvements, fixed pricing, or delivery timelines without approved evidence.
